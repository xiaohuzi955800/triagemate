What TriageMate does

TriageMate is a Forge app for Jira Service Management. It helps support teams triage new requests: a Rovo agent classifies requests, finds the best person to handle each one using the team's work graph, and drafts a first reply. The same expert search is available as an issue panel inside a request.

Data the app processes

When you use TriageMate, the app reads data from your own Atlassian site, acting as you (the signed-in user), and only for the task you asked it to perform:

  • Jira Service Management request details (summary, description, status, priority, reporter, assignee);
  • Workload and team information from the Teamwork Graph (assigned work items, team memberships);
  • The text of your chat messages to the TriageMate agent.

The app does not read data from any other site or service.

Data the app stores

TriageMate stores no personal data. It uses no Forge storage, no database, and no cookies. It does not send data to any external server operated by the developer. All processing happens inside Atlassian's Forge platform and the Rovo service on your own site. When you ask the agent to assign a request or post a reply, it writes that change back to your Jira site — the same way you would yourself.

Third parties

The app calls only Atlassian APIs (Jira, Jira Service Management, Teamwork Graph) within your Atlassian site. Language processing for the Rovo agent runs on Atlassian's infrastructure and is subject to Atlassian's own privacy policy. No other third-party service receives your data from this app.

Your choices

Using TriageMate is optional. Uninstalling the app removes it from your site; because the app stores nothing outside your site, there is no developer-held copy of your data to delete.
